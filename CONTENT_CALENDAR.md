# Gamefluence Content Calendar — Phil from Intender Style

## Strategy: One lead article → weekly deep-dives that each expand a section of the lead

Phil's playbook: The lead article is the **pillar piece** — it establishes the thesis and your authority. Each subsequent article takes ONE section of that pillar and goes deep. This creates:
- Internal linking (each article links back to the pillar + forward to the next)
- SEO compound effect (Google sees you own the topic cluster)
- Social drip (one share per week, never runs dry)
- Retargeting fodder (each article is a reason to re-email your list)
- Sales enablement (every outreach email can link to relevant deep-dive)

## CONTENT RULES (Phil's Intender style)

1. **Every article has ONE clear thesis** — stated in the first paragraph, not buried
2. **Every article has a "so what" for the reader** — what should they DO differently after reading this?
3. **Every article links to at least 2 other articles** — internal link building
4. **Every article has a CTA** — either "Launch a Campaign" or "Talk to Us"
5. **Every article uses specific numbers** — not "we improved UA" but "98% UA growth"
6. **Every article has an enemy** — AI ads, fake influencers, vanity metrics, broken storefronts
7. **Every article positions Joel as the expert** — not through claiming it, through demonstrated knowledge
8. **Publishing cadence: Tuesday mornings** — professionals read industry content Tuesday-Thursday
9. **Social distribution: LinkedIn (long-form excerpt) + Twitter/Bluesky (key stat + link)**
10. **Email list: Each article goes to newsletter subscribers same day**
