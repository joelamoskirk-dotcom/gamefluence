## 10. QUALIFYING INTERVIEW QUESTIONS

These are designed to feel like a casual conversation, not an interrogation. You're at a convention — keep it natural. The goal is to score them on the 5 dimensions above without them feeling "assessed."

### Opening (Build Rapport — 30 seconds)

- "Hey, love your content — what are you playing right now?"
- "Are you here all weekend or just today?"
- "Have you been to Supanova before?"

### Hunger Assessment (Are they looking for income?)

| Question | What Their Answer Tells You |
|----------|----------------------------|
| "Are you doing content full-time or is it a side thing?" | Full-time = hungry for revenue. Side = less urgent but may still want deals. |
| "Have you done any brand deals before?" | None = very hungry. A few = knows the game. Many = may be represented. |
| "How do brand deals usually find you — do they come to you or do you pitch?" | "They come to me" = established. "I pitch" or "I wish" = perfect Gamefluence target. |
| "What's been your best paid collab so far?" | Gives you a rate benchmark. If they say "$200 for a TikTok" they're undervaluing themselves = opportunity. |
| "Would you be open to working with gaming brands from Southeast Asia?" | Direct interest check. Enthusiasm = green light. Hesitation = may need education. |

### APAC Fit Assessment (Do they bridge to SEA markets?)

| Question | What Their Answer Tells You |
|----------|----------------------------|
| "Do you know where most of your audience is from?" | If they say "mostly Australia" that's fine. If they say "heaps from Philippines/Vietnam/Indonesia" = gold. |
| "Do you play any mobile games — Mobile Legends, Free Fire, Genshin?" | Playing SEA-popular titles = natural content fit for APAC campaigns. |
| "Do you speak any other languages or have family overseas?" | Vietnamese/Thai/Filipino heritage = massive APAC bridge potential. |
| "Have you ever thought about growing your audience in Asia?" | Gauges openness. Most mid-tier creators haven't considered it = you're offering something new. |

### Platform & Professionalism Assessment

| Question | What Their Answer Tells You |
|----------|----------------------------|
| "What platforms are you most active on?" | Multi-platform (YT + TikTok + Twitch) = flexible for campaign formats. Single platform = limited. |
| "How often do you post?" | 3+/week = consistent, reliable. Sporadic = risk for campaign timelines. |
| "If a brand sent you a brief tomorrow, how fast could you turn content around?" | 48hrs–1 week = professional. "Whenever" = unreliable. |
| "Do you have a media kit or rate card?" | Yes = professional, knows their worth. No = opportunity to help them build one (value-add from Gamefluence). |

### Growth & Trajectory

| Question | What Their Answer Tells You |
|----------|----------------------------|
| "How's growth been lately — are you seeing your numbers go up?" | Growing = invest now before they get expensive. Flat = may need content strategy help. |
| "What's your goal for the next 12 months with your channel?" | "Go full-time" or "get more brand deals" = perfect alignment. "Just for fun" = lower priority. |
| "Is anyone helping you with your business side — manager, agent, MCN?" | No = unrepresented = sign them. Yes = find out who and whether it's exclusive. |

### The Close (If They Score Well)

| Scenario | What to Say |
|----------|-------------|
| They're keen and unrepresented | "We're building a roster of gaming creators for APAC brand campaigns — paid work, no exclusivity required. Can I grab your email and send you details?" |
| They're interested but cautious | "No pressure — I'll send you a link to check us out. We've got campaigns coming in from Vietnam and Indonesia that need Aussie creators who play [their game]." |
| They're represented | "Totally respect that. If your manager is ever looking for APAC campaign opportunities, happy to chat with them too. Here's my card." |
| They're not a fit | "Awesome chatting — enjoy the rest of Supanova!" (Don't waste time, move on.) |
