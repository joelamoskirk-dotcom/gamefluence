# MOBILEYES — TALENT MANAGEMENT AGREEMENT

**Standard Terms & Conditions v1.0**

Between Mobileyes Pty Ltd ("Mobileyes", "we", "us") and the Talent ("you", "Creator", "Talent") who joins the Mobileyes representation roster.

---

## 1. WHAT MOBILEYES IS

1.1. Mobileyes is an AU-first talent representation agency for live video creators. Think IMG Models, but for streamers and live content creators.

1.2. We represent you to brands and agencies (including the Fabulate pipeline). We handle briefs, negotiate rates, manage logistics, and ensure you get paid fast.

1.3. We are NOT a campaign marketplace. We represent YOU as talent. The relationship is personal, professional, and ongoing.

---

## 2. REPRESENTATION MODEL — NON-EXCLUSIVE

2.1. This Agreement is **non-exclusive**. You are free to:
- Accept work directly from brands you source yourself
- Work with other agencies or platforms
- Decline any Brief we present to you

2.2. However, for any work **sourced by Mobileyes** (i.e., we brought you the opportunity), our commission applies regardless of how the deal is structured.

2.3. If a brand that Mobileyes introduced you to contacts you directly for future work within **12 months** of the original introduction, Mobileyes commission applies unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## 3. COMMISSION — 20%

3.1. Mobileyes retains **20% commission** on all work sourced through our representation.

3.2. You receive **80%** of the agreed service fee.

3.3. Commission applies to:
- Day rates
- Half-day rates
- Per-deliverable fees
- Retainer arrangements sourced by Mobileyes
- Bonus/performance payments tied to Mobileyes-sourced work

3.4. Commission does NOT apply to:
- Work you source entirely independently (no Mobileyes introduction or involvement)
- Existing brand relationships predating this Agreement (must be declared in Schedule A)

---

## 4. PAYMENT — 4 BUSINESS DAYS

4.1. Mobileyes pays Talent within **4 business days** of campaign/service completion verification.

4.2. We do not wait for brand payment before paying you. Once verification confirms delivery, you get paid.

4.3. Payment methods:
- Australian Talent: Bank transfer (BSB/Account)
- International Talent: Wise or PayPal (AUD or USD equivalent)

4.4. Invoicing: Mobileyes invoices the brand/agency on your behalf. You do not need to issue your own invoice unless specifically requested for tax purposes.

4.5. Late payment from brands does not affect your payment timeline. Mobileyes absorbs this risk.

---

## 5. SERVICE DAY RATES

5.1. Your rate card is agreed upon onboarding and reviewed quarterly.

5.2. Standard rate structures:

| Service Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| Full Day Rate | 8+ hours of content/streaming/production |
| Half Day Rate | 4 hours of content/streaming/production |
| Per-Deliverable | Single video, integration, or mention |
| Retainer (Monthly) | Ongoing representation for regular work |

5.3. Rates are set collaboratively. Mobileyes advises on market rates but you approve all quotes before they go to brands.

5.4. Mobileyes will never accept a rate on your behalf without your written confirmation (via e-form or message).

---

## 6. BRIEF ACCEPTANCE — E-FORM

6.1. When a brief comes in that matches your profile, Mobileyes presents it via the Brief Acceptance E-Form.

6.2. The Brief Acceptance E-Form includes:
- Brand name and campaign overview
- Deliverables required
- Platforms (TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, Kick)
- Timeline and deadlines
- Agreed rate (your 80% shown clearly)
- Specific requirements (attribution link/code, talking points, restrictions)
- Terms reference (this Agreement)

6.3. You accept or decline via the e-form. Acceptance constitutes agreement to deliver as specified.

6.4. You must respond within **48 hours** of Brief delivery. Non-response is treated as a decline.

6.5. Once accepted, the brief is binding. Cancellation after acceptance is governed by Section 9.

---

## 7. PLATFORMS & VERIFICATION

7.1. Mobileyes represents talent across:
- **Kick** — Live streaming
- **Twitch** — Live streaming
- **YouTube** — VODs, live streams, shorts
- **TikTok** — Short-form and live

7.2. Campaign completion is verified by Mobileyes technology:

| Requirement | How It's Verified |
|-------------|------------------|
| Content published | URL accessible and returning 200 status |
| Attribution link/code present | Link/code visible in content, description, or chat command |
| Integration correctly placed | Timestamp verification (±10 seconds tolerance) |
| Platform disclosure applied | Paid promotion tag/toggle detected |
| Content duration met | Video/stream duration checked against brief |
| Correct platform used | URL domain matches brief platform |

7.3. Verification triggers payment (4 business days). No subjective review required.

7.4. If verification fails, Mobileyes notifies you within **24 hours** with specific feedback. You have **48 hours** to correct.

---

## 8. FABULATE PIPELINE

8.1. Mobileyes receives briefs through the Fabulate talent marketplace pipeline.

8.2. Fabulate briefs follow the same process: presented via e-form, you accept/decline, verification triggers payment.

8.3. Fabulate may have additional platform-specific requirements — these will be noted in the Brief.

8.4. Commission structure is the same (20%) regardless of brief source.

---

## 9. CANCELLATION & NO-SHOWS

9.1. **You cancel before acceptance:** No consequences. Brief returned to pool.

9.2. **You cancel after acceptance but before delivery date:** Notify Mobileyes immediately. We will attempt to find a replacement. Your reputation score may be affected for future brief matching.

9.3. **You no-show on delivery date:** This is a material breach. Mobileyes may:
- Temporarily suspend brief delivery to you (7-30 days)
- Require a written explanation
- In repeated cases, terminate this Agreement

9.4. **Brand cancels after your acceptance:** You receive 50% of the agreed fee as a cancellation payment within 4 business days.

9.5. **Brand cancels after content created but before publication:** You receive 100% of the agreed fee.

---

## 10. APAC PROTECTION

10.1. For Talent with audiences in APAC markets (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, Taiwan):

10.2. Mobileyes ensures:
- Rates appropriate for the market (not undervalued by global brands unfamiliar with APAC rates)
- Cultural sensitivity in briefs (no content requirements that would alienate local audiences)
- Payment in preferred currency where possible
- Timezone-appropriate deadlines

10.3. APAC talent receives the same 4 business day payment guarantee.

---

## 11. YOUR OBLIGATIONS

11.1. **Quality:** Deliver content that meets Brief requirements.

11.2. **Timeliness:** Publish/stream by the deadline in the Brief.

11.3. **Disclosure:** Comply with advertising disclosure laws (AANA Code, platform tools).

11.4. **Communication:** Respond to briefs within 48 hours. Keep Mobileyes informed of schedule changes.

11.5. **Profile accuracy:** Keep your rate card, platform links, and availability up to date.

11.6. **Brand safety:** Do not produce content that is offensive, illegal, or harmful to the brand's reputation while on a Mobileyes brief.

---

## 12. WHAT MOBILEYES PROVIDES

12.1. Brief sourcing and matching to your profile and rate.

12.2. Rate negotiation with brands/agencies on your behalf.

12.3. All brand communication and logistics management.

12.4. 4 business day guaranteed payment.

12.5. Content verification (so you don't need to chase payment).

12.6. Campaign analytics and performance reporting.

12.7. Quarterly rate review and market rate advice.

12.8. APAC market protection and cultural guidance.

---

## 13. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

13.1. You retain all rights to your content and likeness.

13.2. By completing a brief, you grant the Brand a license as specified in that brief (and only as specified).

13.3. Usage rights beyond the brief require separate negotiation and compensation — Mobileyes manages this on your behalf.

13.4. Mobileyes does not claim ownership of any content you create.

---

## 14. TERMINATION

14.1. Either party may terminate with **14 days written notice** to admin@mobileyes.live.

14.2. Active briefs must be completed — termination does not void accepted work.

14.3. Outstanding payments are made within 14 days of termination.

14.4. The 12-month introduction clause (Section 2.3) survives termination.

---

## 15. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

15.1. The parties agree to attempt to resolve any dispute arising from this Agreement through good-faith negotiation in the first instance.

15.2. If negotiation fails within 14 days, either party may refer the dispute to mediation administered by the Resolution Institute (or equivalent mediation body agreed by both parties).

15.3. Costs of mediation are shared equally.

15.4. If mediation fails within 28 days of referral, either party may commence court proceedings.

15.5. Nothing in this clause prevents a party from seeking urgent interlocutory relief.

---

16.1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of **New South Wales, Australia**.

16.2. The parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of NSW.

16.3. For international talent, local consumer protection laws apply where required by the talent's jurisdiction.

17.1. Mobileyes' total liability is limited to fees earned in the preceding 12 months.

17.2. Mobileyes is not liable for: platform algorithm changes, brand decisions, third-party claims arising from talent's content.

17.3. Talent indemnifies Mobileyes against claims arising from content produced (defamation, copyright, false advertising).
