# Gamefluence Communications Strategy

## Brand Voice
- **Tech/AI-forward**: We lead with our AI matching engine, prediction accuracy, attribution tech
- **APAC gaming specialist**: We know these markets deeply — not a global generalist
- **Data-driven**: Numbers, percentages, ROI metrics in every communication
- **Agency-first language**: "Campaign brief", "attribution", "CPI", "ROAS", "white-label"
- **Confident but not arrogant**: We're building, we're early, but we know more about APAC gaming creators than anyone

## Messaging Hierarchy
1. "We match you with the right creators" (primary — no browsing)
2. "AI-powered, 94.7% prediction accuracy" (tech credibility)
3. "Real attribution across 7 APAC markets" (measurability)
4. "Racing/Sports genre expertise no one else has" (differentiation)
5. "Private roster, vetted creators, guaranteed outcomes" (trust)

## Key Phrases (use consistently)
- "The gaming influencer layer for APAC"
- "AI-matched campaigns"
- "Real attribution"
- "Vietnam → Sydney" (representing the cross-border bridge)
- "We don't share our roster. We match you."
- "280+ verified gaming creators across 7 markets"

## What NOT to say
- Never mention specific creator names publicly (private roster)
- Never say "browse" or "marketplace" (we are NOT a marketplace)
- Never compare directly to competitors by name
- Never use "influencer" alone — always "gaming creator" or "creator"
- Never promise specific ROI numbers to prospects (say "benchmark" or "historical")
- Never mention Mobileyes
