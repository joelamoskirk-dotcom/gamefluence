### The Solution

**Gamefluence** connects brands with verified gaming creators across 7 APAC markets.

We handle matching, briefing, content approval, and attribution — so agencies and brands get measurable results without managing individual creator relationships.

---

### How It Works

```
Brand submits brief → AI matches creators → Creators produce content → Every install attributed
```

1. **Brief** — Market, genre, budget, audience
2. **Match** — AI scores creators on engagement, cultural fit, brand safety
3. **Execute** — Creators produce content on their channels
4. **Report** — Per-creator ROI, CPI, conversion data in real-time
