### Lockup Slide — "Performance Video, Measured"

**Gamefluence** and **Mobileyes** are two sides of the same coin:

| | Gamefluence | Mobileyes |
|---|---|---|
| **What it is** | Gaming creator marketing & attribution platform | AU-first talent agency for live video creators |
| **Who it serves** | Game studios, publishers, agencies, funding bodies | Brands seeking live video creators; creators wanting rep |

When generating any deck from this strategy doc:

1. Use the PROOF POINTS REGISTRY — always link claims to verifiable sources
2. Use the OUTCOME NUMBERS from the Results section — never invent metrics
3. Match the brand voice: Gamefluence = data-driven, Mobileyes = talent-first
4. Every deck ends with a clear CTA and contact info
5. The LOCKUP SLIDE should appear in any deck that mentions both businesses
